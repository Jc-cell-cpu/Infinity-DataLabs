# Infinity DataLabs Brand Guidelines

## Mark

The mark combines a sculpted infinity loop, two endpoint nodes, and paired signal bars. It represents continuous partnership, connected systems, and the movement of data into useful outcomes.

## Logo package

- `infinity-datalabs-icon.svg` — full-color standalone mark
- `infinity-datalabs-favicon.svg` — white favicon mark on midnight navy
- `infinity-datalabs-horizontal-dark.svg` — full-color mark with white wordmark
- `infinity-datalabs-horizontal-light.svg` — full-color mark with dark wordmark
- `infinity-datalabs-horizontal-white.svg` — white monochrome horizontal logo
- `infinity-datalabs-horizontal-black.svg` — black monochrome horizontal logo
- `infinity-datalabs-monochrome.svg` — black monochrome standalone mark
- `infinity-datalabs-vertical.svg` — full-color stacked logo

## Usage

- Keep clear space equal to the height of one internal signal bar around the mark.
- Minimum icon size: 24px digital or 8mm print.
- Use the dark horizontal version on dark surfaces and the light version on bright surfaces.
- Use monochrome variants only when color reproduction is unavailable.
- Never stretch, rotate, recolor individual paths, add drop shadows, or place the logo on busy imagery.

## Wordmark

The wordmark uses a light `Infinity` and a bold `DataLabs`. Inter or Space Grotesk may be used for live digital wordmarks.
