## Overall Impression

A disciplined, genuinely crafted execution of a saturated genre. The two signature moments (nebula, living footer) and the systematic reduced-motion work are real proof-of-craft; the strategy documents are unusually good and the site mostly obeys them. But the page currently fails its own first principle at the infrastructure layer (all content is JS-gated), fails its own AA commitment in the muted range, and gives its brightest object to the wrong CTA. The single biggest opportunity: make the middle of the page as intentional as its edges — right now sections 2–8 are template grammar between two signatures.

## What's Working

1. **The nebula is proof-of-craft, not a library drop-in**: a Gerono figure-eight folded through 3D projection, seeded particles, cyan "signals" becoming violet "systems" through the core — the brand mark, the positioning line, and the hero visual are one idea. DPR-capped, touch-halved, IO-paused, static frame under reduced motion.
2. **Reduced-motion coverage is best-in-class for a marketing page**: Lenis, cursor glow, marquee, counters, nebula, and footer atmosphere all have real fallbacks, verified in source. Most sites fake this with one media query.
3. **Integrity made visible**: no invented logos/metrics/testimonials anywhere; placeholder socials honestly say "profile link pending"; copy stays specific and calm. The restraint system (1px hairlines, Brightest Object, one gradient) is held with unusual discipline.

This fails PRODUCT.md principle 1 ("the site is the proof") at the infrastructure layer and hurts LCP on the exact enterprise/gov networks being courted. **Fix:** visible-by-default, animate additively (IO adds `.in-view`; CSS transitions from offset state inside `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`); at minimum exempt the hero copy block. **Command:** /impeccable harden
