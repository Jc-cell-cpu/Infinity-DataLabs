# Infinity DataLabs Design System

## Direction

Premium AI-first enterprise SaaS. Editorial restraint meets engineering precision. Dark, high-contrast surfaces, subtle spectral light, thin structural lines, generous whitespace, and purposeful motion.

## Tokens

- Canvas: `#05070C`
- Elevated: `#0A0E17`
- Surface: `#101725`
- Text: `#F4F7FB`
- Muted: `#9AA8BC`
- Line: `rgba(255,255,255,.10)`
- Electric blue: `#5B8CFF`
- Cyan: `#58E6FF`
- Violet: `#8B5CF6`
- Success: `#4ADE80`

## Typography

- Display: Space Grotesk variable, tight tracking and compact leading.
- Body/UI: Inter variable, 16px minimum body size, 1.6 line height.
- Eyebrows: 12px uppercase with wide tracking.

## Layout

- Shared max width: 1240px.
- Desktop section rhythm: 112–144px. Mobile: 80–96px.
- Corners: 14px controls, 22px cards, 32px feature surfaces.
- Use asymmetry selectively, with a stable twelve-column underlying grid.

## Motion

- Homepage hero uses a staged 1–3 second reveal with an infinity-shaped field of orbital light trails, luminous particles, and a restrained white-blue energy core.
- Micro interactions: 180–260ms.
- Section reveals: 500–700ms with short travel distance.
- Animate opacity and transforms only.
- Footer uses slow mesh, particle, divider, and outline-watermark motion beneath a static server-rendered information layer.
- Disable smooth scrolling, cursor glow, parallax, and non-essential loops for reduced motion.

## Accessibility

- AA contrast minimum, visible focus rings, 44px minimum targets.
- Semantic landmarks and headings; no interaction depends on hover.
- No decorative element is announced to assistive technology.
- Scrollbars use a slim graphite-to-blue thumb on the canvas track, with a brighter blue hover state and native Firefox support.

## Anti-patterns

- No giant gradient blobs, neon overload, carousels, fake client logos, stock-office imagery, excessive glass, or gratuitous motion.
- No unverified metrics, case-study claims, or named leadership profiles.
